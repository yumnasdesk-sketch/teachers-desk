About Us

The Teacher’s Desk started with a simple idea: every child deserves access to a quality education, and every educator deserves access to the knowledge and resources that can help make that possible.

As an early childhood educator, I created this space to share what I’ve learned and to make useful resources easier for teachers and teaching assistants to find. Everything here is completely free, from practical guides and printables to articles based on research and real educational practice.

I hope The Teacher’s Desk can be a helpful place to find new ideas, save time, and feel a little more prepared for the work that happens in and outside the classroom. Whether you’re a teacher, teaching assistant, or simply someone working with children, I hope you find something here that you can take with you and use.

Yumna Elazrak
Founder & Resource Developer
