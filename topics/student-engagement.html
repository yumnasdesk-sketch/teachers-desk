Improving Student Engagement

Strategies for encouraging participation, curiosity, collaboration, and active learning.

Ask better questions

Use open-ended questions that encourage students to explain their thinking rather than simply recall an answer.

Use varied activities

Mix short explanations with discussion, pair work, practice, demonstrations, and reflection. Variety can help maintain attention.

Give students ownership

Where appropriate, offer choices in examples, topics, or ways to demonstrate learning. Small choices can increase participation.
