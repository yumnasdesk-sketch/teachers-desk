Planning

Simple Lesson Planning

A straightforward approach to planning lessons with clear objectives and activities.

Define the learning objective

Begin by stating what students should know or be able to do by the end of the lesson. A clear objective keeps activities focused.

Plan the lesson flow

Divide the lesson into an introduction, guided learning, practice, and a short review or assessment. Adjust the timing to suit your students.

Leave room to adapt

A plan is a guide, not a script. If students need more practice, slow down. If they demonstrate mastery, move forward.
